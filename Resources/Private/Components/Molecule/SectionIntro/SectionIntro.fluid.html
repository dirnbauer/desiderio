Eyebrow + heading + lead block that opens most content elements. Pass the texts either as arguments (static copy) or as the slots "eyebrow", "heading" and "lead" (inline-editable f:render.text output). `prefix` is the element's BEM block: the eyebrow also carries `{prefix}__eyebrow`, and `headingClass` / `leadClass` add the element's own hooks, so a stylesheet written for those classes keeps applying. {f:if(condition: '{eyebrowContent -> f:format.trim()}', then: eyebrowContent, else: eyebrow)} {f:if(condition: '{headingContent -> f:format.trim()}', then: headingContent, else: heading)} {f:if(condition: '{leadContent -> f:format.trim()}', then: leadContent, else: lead)} {f:if(condition: prefix, then: '{prefix}__')}
