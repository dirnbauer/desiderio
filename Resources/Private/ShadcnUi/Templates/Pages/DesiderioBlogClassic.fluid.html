Page template "Desiderio: Blog – Classic" (backend layout DesiderioBlogClassic). The journal look: a centred masthead, one post after another in a reading column (date leaf, title, picture, teaser) and the blog's widgets in a sidebar. A post keeps the sidebar, opens its text with a drop cap and closes with an ornament instead of a rule. The EXT:blog plugins on these pages render with the templates in Resources/Private/Extensions/Blog/Classic/ first and fall back to the shared ones in Resources/Private/Extensions/Blog/ (see the webconsulting/desiderio-blog set). Styles: .desiderio-blog--classic in Resources/Private/Css/desiderio/23-blog.css. A blog post is its own doktype-137 page (BlogPageTreeSeeder::BLOG_POST_DOKTYPE). Category and tag helper folders sit next to the posts, so posts are told apart by doktype alone, never by their storage folder.
