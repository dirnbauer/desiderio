Page template "Desiderio: Blog – Modern" (backend layout DesiderioBlogModern). The magazine look: the newest post as a large card, the others in a card grid, the blog's topics as pills above them and no sidebar. A post opens with a wide picture and reads in one column next to its table of contents, with a reading progress bar at the top of the window. The EXT:blog plugins on these pages render with the templates in Resources/Private/Extensions/Blog/Modern/ first and fall back to the shared ones in Resources/Private/Extensions/Blog/ (see the webconsulting/desiderio-blog set). Styles: .desiderio-blog--modern in Resources/Private/Css/desiderio/23-blog.css. A blog post is its own doktype-137 page (BlogPageTreeSeeder::BLOG_POST_DOKTYPE). Category and tag helper folders sit next to the posts, so posts are told apart by doktype alone, never by their storage folder.
